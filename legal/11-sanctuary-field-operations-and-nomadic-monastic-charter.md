## ARTICLE II — IRS ACCOUNTABLE PLAN FOR FIELD RESEARCH, TRAVEL, LODGING & CONNECTIVITY (`IRC § 62(a)(2)(A)` & `TREAS. REG. § 1.62-2`)

To ensure that field expenses, temporary sanctuary lodging, meals during travel, and mobile connectivity incurred by Foundation Officers, Directors, Researchers, or Volunteers on Foundation business are reimbursed **100% tax-free** (excluded from gross income and never treated as private inurement or taxable wages), the Board of Directors hereby adopts this **IRS-Compliant Accountable Plan** under `IRC § 62(a)(2)(A)` and `Treasury Regulation § 1.62-2`:

### Section 2.1 The Three Mandatory Accountable Plan Rules (`Treas. Reg. § 1.62-2(c)`)

Every reimbursement or direct corporate-card payment under this Charter shall strictly satisfy all three federal statutory conditions:

1. **Exempt Business / Mission Connection (`Treas. Reg. § 1.62-2(d)`):**
   - The expense must be incurred while performing religious, scientific, educational, or charitable duties for **The Sovereign Constellation Foundation**, including:
     - **Field Lodging & Temporary Sanctuary Stays:** Hotel, hostel, guesthouse, or short-term rental lodging while traveling away from one's tax home for contemplative field research, monastery/temple visits, open-source engineering sprints, or conference/community outreach;
     - **Subsistence & Meals on Travel Status:** Reasonable daily meals and hydration while on Foundation travel or field research status (reimbursable either via actual receipts or at the standard **U.S. GSA / Department of State International Per Diem M&IE rate** for the city of travel, e.g., Tokyo, Kyoto, Vienna, New York, San Francisco);
     - **Multi-Device Connectivity & Edge Compute Field Supplies:** Mobile Wi-Fi hotspots, eSIM/data plans, portable power banks, cables, and low-power CPU/microcontroller test hardware required to keep the `8b-is` open-source research mesh operational in the field; and
     - **Transit & Relocation:** Rail, bus, airfare, and local transit between Field Sanctuary nodes.
2. **Timely Substantiation Within 60 Days (`Treas. Reg. § 1.62-2(e)`):**
   - Within **sixty (60) days** after an expense is paid or incurred, the researcher/officer must submit a digital log entry (`SHA-256` receipt photo or GSA Per Diem travel log) stating:
     - *(a)* The amount and date of the expense (or dates/location for GSA Per Diem);
     - *(b)* The place of the field sanctuary or travel; and
     - *(c)* The specific Foundation exempt purpose (e.g., *"Field testing `Aurora-8B-Ultra-RL v0.5.0` multi-device voice mesh & compiling Tibetan-Hungarian Mahākāla Pecha in Tokyo Field Sanctuary"*).
3. **Return of Any Excess Advance Within 120 Days (`Treas. Reg. § 1.62-2(f)`):**
   - If the Foundation provides a travel or field-sanctuary advance, any unsubstantiated excess must be returned to the Foundation Treasury within **one hundred twenty (120) days**.
